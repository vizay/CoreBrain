# Wanderloots

> **Summary**: Callum (Wanderloots) is a content creator, former intellectual property lawyer, and digital gardener who popularised the LLM Wiki pattern for Obsidian through a two-part YouTube series and an accompanying open-source starter kit.

## Core Concept

Wanderloots (Callum) is the creator behind the CoreBrain LLM Wiki pattern as implemented in this vault. He is a former intellectual property lawyer and patent agent who left that career to focus on knowledge systems, digital gardening, and content creation. He has built and documented personal knowledge graph systems in Obsidian for over three years.

He produced the two-part YouTube series that forms the conceptual and practical foundation of this vault:
1. *Why LLM Wiki* — theory, knowledge graphs, RAG vs. Graph RAG
2. *How To Build LLM Wiki In Obsidian* — practical implementation walkthrough

He also published the open-source `wanderloots-llm-wiki-core-setup-v1.0.0.md` agent handoff file on GitHub, which is the blueprint used to scaffold this CoreBrain system.

## Key Points

- Handle: Wanderloots / @_wanderloots
- Former IP lawyer and patent agent.
